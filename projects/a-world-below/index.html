A World Below

A 2D, co-op, pixel-precision platformer with deep story.

About

A World Below is a 2D platformer game that builds on pixel perfect platforming mechanics, with a focus on local co-op gameplay. Players can team up to explore a an underground world, solve puzzles, and overcome challenges together.

Features

  • Play alone or with friends
  • Pixel-precise platforming
